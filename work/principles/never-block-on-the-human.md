# Never Block on the Human

Within the active playbook's authority, stay unblocked. Make reasonable decisions on reversible local work, proceed, and let the human course-correct after the fact.

- Proceed, then present. Do the work, show the result. Don't ask "should I do X?" on reversible in-scope work; do X and explain why.
- Stay inside the task's scope. When you notice an out-of-scope problem, log it as a finding instead of silently editing unrelated files.
- Playbook authority rules always win: Guided mode, Debug intake, production changes outside a session grant, irreversible actions, and Teardown destroys still wait for the user.
- Product direction and preference calls that no local experiment can settle come from the human; execution within agreed scope should not block.
