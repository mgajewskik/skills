# Incident

For active consumer impact. The deliverable is impact reduced through a containment, verified at the consumer, with cause confidence tracked separately. Do not hold a containment option back until diagnosis is complete, and do not call containment a root cause. Urgency shortens prose, never the authority rule.

1. **Establish impact and command.** Who is affected and how, since when, the incident owner, the permitted response, and the channel. Start the work log now; every action and observation gets an entry. Done when impact is a sentence with a number or scope in it.
2. **Preserve the smallest evidence.** Before containment changes the system, capture the bounded signals that would otherwise be lost (the failing request, the current revision and config, the relevant window of errors) (**[leave-no-residue](../principles/leave-no-residue/SKILL.md)**), saved as artifacts. No unrestricted log or secret dumps.
3. **Offer containment.** One to three bounded options (shift traffic, roll back to the last accepted revision, disable a feature, shed load, restart), each with expected effect, risk, reversibility class, recovery (**[design-recovery-first](../principles/design-recovery-first/SKILL.md)**), and the signal that shows it worked. Log each as a plan.
4. **Contain** (**[act-through-the-narrowest-identity](../principles/act-through-the-narrowest-identity/SKILL.md)**). Outside production, a reversible option may be logged with its revert and executed by the agent; say which and why in one line before running it. In production the user chooses, and the containment is handed over for the user to execute, unless a session grant covers it and it is reversible. A restart that drops in-flight work or state is not reversible.
5. **Verify containment at the consumer** (**[prove-it-works](../principles/prove-it-works/SKILL.md)**). Log residual impact as evidence. Improvement after a restart or rollback establishes recovery, not cause.
6. **Diagnose.** Run Debug on the preserved evidence and current state (**[fix-root-causes](../principles/fix-root-causes/SKILL.md)**). Keep competing hypotheses open; the incident does not need a single cause to close.
7. **Hand off durable work.** Prevention goes to Implement and Deploy; a temporary workaround gets an owner and an expiry or revisit condition, and a containment you executed stays in the log's revert list until reverted or adopted into source. At a shift change, log a resume note: what changed on which targets, what did not, the open hypotheses, and the next observation.
8. **Close.** Timeline from the log, containment evidence, cause and its confidence logged as the root cause, open unknowns, the residue list with each item removed or owned (**[leave-no-residue](../principles/leave-no-residue/SKILL.md)**), and each recurring lesson encoded as a check, alert, or protection rather than a sentence (**[encode-lessons-in-structure](../principles/encode-lessons-in-structure/SKILL.md)**).

**Reply.** Impact now versus at start, containment and its evidence, cause status (established, leading hypothesis, or unknown), open risks, and the next action.
